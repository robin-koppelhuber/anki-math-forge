{# A list of commands to copy. Four places render one: the triage rail, the setup stage's column, the panel that creates a topic, and the picker's import corner. They had drifted into four spellings of the same markup, which is how one of them ends up without the hover that says where to paste it. Copied, never launched. You paste it where you can watch it, so nothing writes cards with nobody looking. That is the whole bargain, and it is why these are buttons that put text on a clipboard rather than controls that run something. **Two kinds, and they are not equally safe to paste.** An info command shows you something and an action command changes a file, and the difference is the whole reason these are copied rather than launched, so it is worth more than a sentence in a tooltip. `reads` marks the safe half, they are grouped apart, and the heading says which is which.
