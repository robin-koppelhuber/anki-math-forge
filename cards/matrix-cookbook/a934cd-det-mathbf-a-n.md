## front
$\det(\mathbf{A}^n)$

## back
$\det(\mathbf{A})^n$

## conditions
$\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$; $n \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$, or $n \in \mathbb{Z}_{<0}$ with $\mathbf{A}$ invertible.
