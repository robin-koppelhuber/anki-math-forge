## front
$\frac{\partial \mathbf{a}^T\mathbf{x}}{\partial \mathbf{x}}$

## back
$\mathbf{a}$

## conditions
Denominator layout.

## prose
$\mathbf{a}^T\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{x}^T\mathbf{a}$ are the same scalar, so
both differentiate to $\mathbf{a}$.
