## front
$\text{Tr}(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B})$

## back
$\text{Tr}(\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A})$

## conditions
$\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$; $\mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$.

## prose
Neither factor need be square; only the two products are.
