## front
$(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}\mathbf{C}\cdots)^H$

## back
$\cdots\mathbf{C}^H\mathbf{B}^H\mathbf{A}^H$

## conditions
$\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C}, \ldots$ complex and conformable, so that the product is defined. None of them need be square.
