## front
$\det(c\mathbf{A})$ for $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$

## back
$c^n \det(\mathbf{A})$

## conditions
$c \in \mathbb{R}$.

## prose
The exponent is the dimension of $\mathbf{A}$: scaling by $c$ scales the determinant by $c^n$, not by $c$.
