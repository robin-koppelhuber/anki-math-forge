## front
$\det(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B})$

## back
$\det(\mathbf{A})\det(\mathbf{B})$

## conditions
$\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$.

## prose
Both factors must be square: for $\mathbf{A}$ $m \times n$ and $\mathbf{B}$ $n \times m$ the left-hand side is defined and the right-hand side is not.
