## front
$\partial(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{Y})$

## back
$(\partial\mathbf{X})\mathbf{Y} + \mathbf{X}(\partial\mathbf{Y})$

## conditions
$\partial$ the differential; $\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{Y}$ differentiable matrix functions of a common variable.

## prose
The scalar product rule with each factor left where it stood, because the matrix
product does not commute.
